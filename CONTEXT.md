# Personal Academic Website

This website presents Zhen Hou's academic profile, publications, and teaching experience.

## Language

**Teaching Entry**:
A record of Zhen Hou's role in teaching a named course during specified semesters.
_Avoid_: Course offering, faculty appointment

**Course Page**:
The detailed presentation of a teaching entry and the learning materials associated with that course.
_Avoid_: Teaching index, personal profile

**Notes**:
Tutorial notes that review course concepts and include exercises, hints, or explanatory remarks.
_Avoid_: Lecture slides, solution manual

**Problem Set**:
A numbered collection of course exercises; hints and remarks do not make it a complete solution set.
_Avoid_: Answer key, notes
